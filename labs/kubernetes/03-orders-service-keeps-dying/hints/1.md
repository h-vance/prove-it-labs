The customer has told you two things that sound like exclusions and are not.
"It is only a config value, not code" and "nobody has deployed in two weeks"
both assume that only code changes can cause a crash. Plenty of things stop a
process that have nothing to do with its source.

The distinction worth proving first is bigger than which setting changed:

> Did the application fail, or was it stopped?

Those look identical from the outside. Both produce restarts, both produce user
errors, both recover. They have completely different causes and completely
different fixes, and the evidence that separates them is recorded on the pod
itself.

Also note the timing the customer gave you. Recovering and failing on a cycle
suggests something is being hit repeatedly, not something that broke once.
