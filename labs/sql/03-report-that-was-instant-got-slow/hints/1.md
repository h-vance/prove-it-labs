The customer has told you the most useful thing in the ticket without meaning
to: nothing changed except the amount of data. That rules out the query, the
schema, and any recent deploy, and it points at how the work scales rather than
what the work is.

Resist timing it. A stopwatch tells you what happened once, on this machine,
with this cache state, at today's size. It cannot tell you what the customer
actually asked, which is where this ends up in six months.

The database can tell you exactly how it intends to answer the question, before
and separately from how long that took. Ask it that.
