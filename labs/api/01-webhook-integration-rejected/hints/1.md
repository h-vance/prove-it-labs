The customer has handed you a conclusion, not a symptom. "We assume your API is
having problems" is a theory, and confirming it without checking is how support
engineers lose credibility.

From inside the customer's logs, a service outage and a refused request look
identical: the call failed. From your side they are completely different, and
one command separates them.

Reproduce the call and look at the **status line** before you look at anything
else. If the API were down, you would not get a status line at all.
