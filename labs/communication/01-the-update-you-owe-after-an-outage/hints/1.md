Read the draft you were given and ask who it was written for.

It is not wrong about the facts. Every technical claim in it is true. It is
written for somebody who already knows how the system is put together, and it
is being sent to somebody who has told you plainly that they cannot use that.

The customer asked four questions in their ticket, and they are the outline:

1. What happened, in terms they can repeat to their leadership.
2. How long they were affected.
3. Whether their data is wrong now.
4. Whether it will happen again.

The draft answers roughly half of one of them.

Start from what they could not do rather than from what broke. Those are
different sentences, and only the first one is checkable by the person reading
it. "The customer list would not load for 47 minutes" is something they can
verify against their own logs. "The connection setting was wrong" is something
they have to take on faith.
